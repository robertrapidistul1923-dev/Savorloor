Save food. Save money. Waste less.

Small changes can make a big difference.

Savorloop is a simple food-waste awareness project designed to help households make better use of the food they already have.

See simple tips

What is Savorloop?

Savorloop encourages people to reduce avoidable food waste through planning, correct storage, portion awareness and creative use of leftovers.

The idea is simple: use more of what you buy, throw away less, and make everyday food choices a little more sustainable.

Easy ways to reduce food waste

📝

Plan before shopping

Check what you already have and make a short shopping list before buying more.

🥕

Use older food first

Keep food that needs using soon at the front of your fridge or cupboard.

🍲

Reuse leftovers

Turn leftover ingredients into soups, sandwiches, pasta dishes or simple lunches.

❄️

Freeze what you can

Freeze suitable food before it goes out of date instead of throwing it away.

Why it matters

Less waste means better use of food, money and resources.

1. Buy only what you need.
2. Store food properly.
3. Use leftovers creatively.
4. Waste less over time.

About this project
